Heart of the Beast

2026 · Action / Drama / Adventure / Thriller

After a disastrous plane crash, a special forces soldier and his service dog find themselves isolated in the Alaskan wilderness. A challenging struggle for survival awaits them in a harsh environment, where every day presents new and dangerous challenges. They must find ways to cope with unpredictable weather and the wild wilderness to preserve life in this unforgiving and remote environment.
