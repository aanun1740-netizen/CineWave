Peaceful Warrior

2006 · Sport / Drama

Dan Millman is a promising college athlete and aspiring Olympian, living a life full of accolades, friendships, motorcycles, attention from the opposite sex, and fun parties. However, everything changes when he meets a mysterious man nicknamed Socrates, who possesses knowledge capable of illuminating the path to deep self-knowledge and inner strength. Faced with a brutal trauma, Dan, with the support of Socrates and the attractive and enigmatic Joy, begins to realize that his education is far from complete, and new horizons of wisdom and self-understanding open up before him.

Столкнувшись с жестокой травмой, Дэн, благодаря поддержке Сократа и привлекательной и загадочной Джой, начинает осознавать, что его обучение далеко от завершения, и перед ним открываются новые горизонты мудрости и понимания себя.
