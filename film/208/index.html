Creed: Rocky's Legacy

2015 · Drama/Sport

Adonis Johnson grew up barely knowing his father or mother, spending his childhood in foster homes and juvenile institutions. His life took a turning point when he was found and adopted by Mary Ann, the widow of legendary boxer Apollo Creed. Adonis learned the truth: he was the illegitimate son of a long-disrespected champion. As an adult, he chose not a quiet career but the ring and underground fights, dreaming of achieving everything on his own and not even taking his father's name. Mary Ann was against it: she had seen how boxing had destroyed Apollo. Unable to find support from trainers in Los Angeles, Adonis traveled to Philadelphia to join Rocky Balboa, Creed's longtime rival and friend, to start over and become a true fighter.
