The Invite

2026 · New Releases / Comedy

Joe and Angela haven't experienced the same intense emotions in their relationship for a long time. Hoping to spice things up, Angela decides to invite her neighbors, who are known for their passionate love, to dinner. The sounds coming from their apartment hint at unbridled passion, in contrast to the silence in Joe and Angela's home. Expectations for the evening are dashed when the neighbors make an unexpected proposal, upending any notion of a normal social gathering. This adds tension and an unexpected twist to the friendly get-together.
