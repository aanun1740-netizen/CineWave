Crime Scene Investigation

2026 · Drama/Comedy

Behind the scenes of an ambitious production of the opera "The Marriage of Figaro," tension reigns: rehearsals are at breaking point, and tensions within the company are growing. A high-profile accusation of sexual assault is unexpectedly uncovered, threatening not only the reputations of individuals but also the fate of the entire production. The investigation unearths hidden conflicts and long-held secrets, shattering traditional alliances and dividing the company into warring camps. Each member is forced to decide what is most important: preserving the production at any cost, protecting their careers and reputation, or acting according to their conscience, even if it means consequences.
