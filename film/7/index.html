Dangerous Duet

2026 · Action · Comedy

Johnny and James, half-brothers who haven't spoken for twenty years due to deep-seated differences, are forced to put aside old grievances and work together after their father's death. They face the task of solving the mystery of his murder, trying to understand who could be behind it. During the investigation, the brothers not only search for the killer but also rethink their past relationships, trying to find ways to reconcile and understand each other. Their adventure is full of tense moments and unexpected twists that help them grow closer.
