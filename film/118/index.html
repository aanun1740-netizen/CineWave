Family Affair

2023 · Romance / Comedy

We meet a woman named Zara, terribly tired of her boss. Chris, a Hollywood star, behaves like a complete narcissist and regularly reduces his assistant to tears. He also has a penchant for every woman, giving the impression of being an incorrigible womanizer. After his latest outburst, Zara realizes she can't go on like this. She decides to quit and start looking for a more secure job. Everything changes dramatically when she makes an incredible discovery. Zara learns that Chris is having an affair with her own widowed mother, which leaves her in shock. Why would a famous actor need an older woman? And why would her mother even date a man who regularly finds himself at the center of scandals? Incredible days are coming, ones that will change Zara's life forever.

И почему мать вообще начала встречаться с человеком, который регулярно оказывается в центре скандалов? Грядут невероятные дни, что изменят жизнь Зары раз и навсегда.
