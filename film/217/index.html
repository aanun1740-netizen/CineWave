Miami Frozen

2009 · Comedy / Romance

Lucy Hill adores elegant outfits, lusts behind the wheel of luxury cars, and strives to climb the professional ladder. Suddenly, she's transferred from the scorching Miami sun to freezing Minnesota with a work assignment. Faced with an unwelcoming climate and a cool reception from the region's residents, who are reluctant to welcome the newcomer, Lucy must learn to navigate unfamiliar challenges to achieve her goal.
