A sci-fi action film about a young blogger, Franklin, who accidentally saves the life of a renowned visionary and medical innovator. Immersed in his investigation, he acts increasingly boldly, barely considering the consequences. But the artifact has a true owner, and when the owner realizes the ring is missing, a hunt begins.
