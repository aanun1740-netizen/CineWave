Peaky Blinders: The Immortal Man

2026 · Historical / Crime / Drama / Movies / New items

During World War II, Tommy Shelby finds himself back in Birmingham, scarred by air strikes and struggling. The city he once controlled has changed—as has he. His return quickly turns from an attempt to start anew into a series of dangerous decisions: Tommy is drawn into classified operations where any mistake could be costly. Against the backdrop of war, he faces new enemies and hidden threats emanating not only from without but also from within his own circle. Simultaneously, Shelby struggles to come to terms with the ghosts of the past and understand the price he must pay for his growing responsibility to his country.
