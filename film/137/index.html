Resident Evil

2026 · Horror

A courier who often faces challenges at work is tasked with delivering a package to a remote medical facility. However, his mission quickly turns into a nightmare when he finds himself at the center of a mysterious virus outbreak. Amidst utter chaos, the virus-contaminated facility becomes the arena for a brutal fight for survival. The courier must not only withstand the relentless onslaught of mutated monsters but also find a way to escape the infected zone.
