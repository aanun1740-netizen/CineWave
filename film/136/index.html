Operation Fortune: The Art of Winning

2022 · Thriller / Comedy / Action

An elite mercenary named Jacob Fortune is vacationing abroad on a paradise island. He's enjoying a well-deserved break when the British government suddenly contacts him. Overnight, reliable sources reveal that an eccentric billionaire arms dealer has acquired secret technology and intends to sell the deadly weapon to dangerous gangsters. The criminal gang, having obtained the mysterious device, could easily seize power not only over the city, but over the entire world. They're ready to wipe humanity off the face of the earth, and only the experienced Fortune can stop them. But Jacob can't do it alone. He's forced to enlist the help of the world's best operatives. And a renowned actor will be helping save innocent lives. But will the spies be able to play by Hollywood's rules and prevent disaster, or will Jacob make a fatal mistake?

А помогать спасать невинных людей будет помогать именитый актер. Но удастся ли шпионам сыграть по правилам Голливуда и предотвратить катастрофу или Джейкоб совершит роковую ошибку?
